Site Plan: Naija Premier League Hub

Site Name

Naija Premier League Hub

This name clearly reflects the purpose of the project—providing an online hub that highlights leading clubs in the Nigerian Premier Football League (NPFL). It is simple, memorable, and immediately communicates the site’s focus.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide fans and visitors with clear, engaging, and reliable information about three major NPFL teams: Enyimba FC, Remo Stars FC, and Kano Pillars FC. The site will feature club history, achievements, profiles, fixtures, and interactive elements built with JavaScript.

Scenarios

  • “I want to know the history and major achievements of Enyimba, Remo Stars, and Kano Pillars.”
  • “Which NPFL club has the most league titles?”

Color Schema

The website will use two primary colors:

  • Deep Blue (#0b3d91): Used for headings, navigation bar, and accents.
  • Green (#07a50c): Used for highlights, buttons, and emphasis elements.

These colors complement football themes, provide strong contrast, and maintain visual clarity across devices.

Typography

The site will use the following fonts:

  • Lato – Used for all body text, headings, navigation, and interactive areas.

Lato is clean, modern, legible, and aligns with previous class projects for consistency.
